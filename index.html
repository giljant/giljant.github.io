Case study2026Construction

LTG a general contractor, built to earn quote requests

LTG is a Croatian construction company with more than 30 years of work, from shell construction and facades to turnkey handover. They wanted one site that shows the full range of what they do, proves they can be trusted with large buildings, and turns visitors into quote requests.

Role
Website design
Tool
Figma
Scope
Desktop + mobile
Shown
Homepage, Facade services
Scroll inside the screens Open full page ↗

What I did

  1. Built the homepage around their promise of one contractor and one point of responsibility, with a dusk site photo and three proof points right in the hero.
  2. Sorted six trades into photo cards so each service leads to its own page.
  3. Designed the facade page as the template for every service page: a comparison of ETICS, ventilated and aluminium-glass systems, reasons to choose LTG, references, FAQ and a short quote form.
  4. Took the palette from the job site itself: charcoal, concrete grey and a rust orange used only for calls to action.
  5. Laid out mobile separately: services in a two-column grid, projects stacked, a one-tap menu and the same form at the end of every page.
02

Case study2026Education

STEM-online tutoring pages that answer local searches

STEM-online connects school and university students with tutors from Zagreb’s top technical faculties, in person or online. They needed pages that answer local searches like “maths tutoring in Zagreb” and make booking a tutor take as few steps as possible.

Role
Website design
Tool
Figma
Scope
Landing page system
Shown
Maths tutoring, Zagreb; Tutoring across Croatia
Scroll inside the screens Open full page ↗

What I did

  1. Designed one landing page template that works for every subject and city combination, with breadcrumbs that mirror the URL path for search engines.
  2. Gave each city a local hero: the Zagreb coat of arms surrounded by maths symbols, in the brand’s violet on deep navy.
  3. Designed tutor cards with faculty and neighbourhood tags, so a student can judge a match at a glance.
  4. Built the Croatia overview page around a city picker and a four-step booking explainer for in-person and online lessons.
  5. Kept the tutor grid at two columns on mobile and moved the neighbourhood map below the booking button.

Process

From brief to launch

  1. Brief

    Talk with the client about the business, the audience and what the site has to achieve.

  2. Structure

    Turn their raw content into a sitemap and a clear order of sections for each page.

  3. Design

    Design desktop and mobile in Figma, present it, and iterate through feedback rounds.

  4. Build

    Build in HTML, CSS and React, matching the design down to spacing and type.

  5. Test & launch

    Check across devices and browsers, tune SEO and load speed, deploy, then maintain.
